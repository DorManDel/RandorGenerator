Security boundary

Key Studio

Passwords, secure tokens and browser-managed AES-GCM keys. Camera signals are deliberately excluded from the security-critical path.

Important: this is a learning prototype. Browser cryptographic primitives are established; the surrounding application has not been independently security-audited. Do not rely on an experimental key format for production.
Future research laneNOT ENABLED

Structured keys, HKDF-derived subkeys, key/IV encoding studies and bitfield visualizers belong to a separate research sandbox with known security properties and a documented threat model. IV/nonce rules remain algorithm-specific.
